How TrustMail works

Learn what TrustMail checks

TrustMail checks different aspects separately instead of combining everything into one “safe” score.

  • Sender identity: What the sender claims, and what identity evidence is available.
  • Message authenticity: What can be established about the message’s origin and integrity.
  • Threat level: Warning signs such as payment redirection, password requests or pressure tactics.
  • Sender history: What this local prototype knows about the sender from available history.
Human review: Serious or ambiguous findings may need a person to investigate. TrustMail does not make the final decision for you.
